### Verdict

**FAIL**

The change is well-built and honestly documented, and I could not break the renderer, the marker rule, the digest rule or the age threshold — each resisted a mutation. What blocks the archive is narrower than the diff: the step reads the scheduler before it reads the opt-in, so a machine that never asked for self-deploy inherits a hard converge failure wherever the native scheduler cannot be reached. That is the exact failure class this spec's own mechanism is supposed to make loud-but-harmless, the cited prior art (`workstationTarget`) does it the other way round, doctor in this same diff does it the other way round, and the bats case that used to guard "non-fatal" was deleted without replacing the property.
