### Verdict

**FAIL**

The mechanism is sound and the queue/parent/argv design does what the proposal says — I watched it decide correctly against the live forge and the live herdr. It fails the gate on the last metre: a user cannot see *why* a lane command refused (Blocker, reproduced four ways), AC5's no-backend promise does not hold for the plain invocation (reproduced), and the printed no-backend commands would create a worktree this same release's `dotf worktree done` cannot find under its branch (reproduced against `dotf worktree add`).
