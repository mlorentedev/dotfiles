### Verdict

**FAIL**

This is a good change with a bad instrument bolted to it. The retirement is real and complete — I could not find a live router, a record, a vault pattern or a deployed surface that still names a retired skill, the folds kept every rule I went looking for, severities survived as specified, the routing repair is demonstrably load-bearing, and the whole thing is still green after merging current `main`. What fails is f6, the sole re-verification of AC6 across future rounds: it reports clean when it inspected nothing, and on the platform whose quirk the neighbouring script already documents and defends against, it inspects nothing **always**. The skill's own doctrine names that shape — an instrument reporting green because it measured the wrong object — and this spec has already spent two rounds discovering that about this check.

**Minimum set that flips this to PASS** (all outside the contract set, so no round is wasted re-litigating the acceptance criteria):

1. `scripts/check-retired-skills.sh` fails closed: require jq and a readable manifest (mirror `compile-harness.sh`'s `require_tools`), neutralise CRLF output the way that script's `jq()` wrapper does, and exit 2 when either manifest target list yields nothing.
2. `tests/check-retired-skills.bats`: one case per arm — CR-emitting jq, emptied `.skills.deploy`, unreadable manifest — each asserting a non-zero exit with a marked copy present. Red before the fix, green after (red-green verified here: the current suite passes with zero targets).
3. `TestEverySkillTheRouterNamesHasARecord`: add the records' `requires:` in both YAML styles (finding 2). Leave `features.json` alone.
4. Re-run f1–f6, `go test ./...` and the five bats files; then re-review at the new head.

Findings 3–8 need a disposition line in `verification.md` (applied, ticketed, or declined with a reason), not a fix, to travel with a passing verdict. Finding 6 is a human call about `warn` noise: keep it and say why, or flatten it and accept that the reviewer's third obligation stops being gated.
